You asked him what he wants. He said, "Nothing, I'm fine." You asked again a week later and got the same answer, with a small shrug this time.

Every year it goes like this. And every year you end up at the same shortlist: a shirt he might not wear, a perfume he already has, or cash in an envelope that feels like you gave up.

There is a better answer, and it sits on his wrist.

## Why a bracelet works for the man who wants nothing

Men who say they want nothing usually mean something specific. They don't want clutter. They don't want a thing that needs batteries, a manual or a drawer to live in.

A bracelet is the opposite of clutter. It is small, it goes everywhere he goes, and it says something about him without him having to say it. He will notice it when he reaches for his phone, when he shakes a hand, when he rests his chin on his fist in a meeting.

![A bracelet of dark and metallic beads resting on a rock at sunset](/images/final-cta-bracelet-on-a-mountain.webp "A good bracelet should look at home anywhere he goes.")

The trick is choosing one that matches who he already is, not who you wish he would dress like. So start with him.

## How to get his size without asking

Asking "what is your wrist size?" ruins the surprise. Here is how to find it quietly.

1. **Borrow a watch he already wears.** Lay the strap flat and measure from the end of the buckle to the hole he uses most. That is close to his wrist size.
2. **No watch? Use string.** If he is asleep or distracted, loop a piece of string around his wrist, mark it, and measure it against a ruler later.
3. **Add a little room.** Add 1 to 2 cm to the measurement for a comfortable fit.

Then match it to our sizes: Small (16 cm), Medium (18 cm) or Large (20 cm). Still not sure? Send us a WhatsApp message with your best guess and we will help you choose.
